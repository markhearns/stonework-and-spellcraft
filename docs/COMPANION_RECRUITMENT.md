# Named companion recruitment in v0.118

Introductions, visits and household membership are separate decisions. Except where an authored chapter supplies its own meeting, continue from **People & arrivals**, discuss the available topics, provide suitable accommodation, Advance for an agreed visit, and ask about staying before extending a household invitation.

Merrin is included in **Cheats → Instantly recruit unique companions → All remaining unique companions** and can also be selected individually. Cheats skip recruitment without completing her chapters, quests, romance or wardrobe invitations.

## Merrin's wardrobe

**Appearance & wardrobe** on her character profile shows her clothing. Her everyday chapel-keeper portrait is available immediately. **Plum evening dress** is her second (relaxed) outfit, offered after one remembered shared moment. **Plum halter and wrap skirt** is her third (daring) outfit: a cropped halter top and short wrap skirt with exposed shoulders and midriff. It requires the relaxed invitation, three distinct remembered moments, and at least one new moment after that first invitation. Accepting an invitation unlocks an outfit; selecting it is a separate action. Both people must be home.

The lilac bathing outfit is separate. It appears during her personal time in a restored pool, sauna or hot spring, then returns to her chosen everyday outfit when she leaves or starts work. Outfits confer no numeric bonuses and do not establish romance.

## New procedural residents

After Chapter 4, **Recruitment quests & contacts** offers one-phase report gathering. Rescue or bandit leads save an identity and must be completed before an invitation. Reports usually feature common ancestries, with a 1% exotic chance. Golems and spirits never appear in either pool. Dryads and nymphs appear only in rescues. Elementals have four illustrated variants.

For exotic magical contacts, prepare a saved invitation and complete the normal paid summoning work. Golems require construction and awakening. Custom identity authoring is a Cheat. **Portrait description** is only optional appearance text for future prompts; saving it does not change or generate a portrait.
