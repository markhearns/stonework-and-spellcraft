# Character art direction

All resident artwork should depict youthful, clearly adult characters. Preserve each established identity, ancestry features and adult proportions. Avoid older-looking facial treatment without making faces childlike or identical. Outfit progression changes dress and expression, not apparent age.

Never depict high-heeled shoes or boots. Use flat practical soles, flat sandals or bare feet as appropriate; no wedges or platforms. Check both feet, including side views, in every outfit and format.

Each resident has three full-body transparent portraits and three conversational overview illustrations. Overview backgrounds belong to the resident's associated room (Kaede: training yard; Brakka: forge; Velis: Supply Office). Cutouts require real alpha transparency, with the entire head, ancestry features and feet inside the frame. Preserve the established engraved fantasy style and outfit continuity.

Review generated images visually, verify alpha on a contrasting background, then optimize to WebP. Keep generation prompts and source filenames in the release art manifest. Never overwrite user-accepted artwork stored in a save.
