LIFE AT HOME
24 authored scenes: two each for the eleven specialists and demonstration resident Mira. First choices are remembered and echoed in follow-ups. Follow-ups require actual shared research work, or one shared review phase of a personally known common principle. Merely assigning a character after the project is already complete does not grant participation. Shared reviews restore prior assignments only for the next phase, preventing double work.
Optional invitations do not expire, spend time, grant resources or force relationship changes. Phase/day routines only select available rooms and never override actual work or away states. Resting residents have brief ambient greetings.

NAVIGATION

Specialist absence pauses installations but does not disable cancellation/refunds. Recruitment guidance points to the actual study or expedition required.
