# Development v0.56 — Authored magic

Thirty fixed spells, eight lasting rituals, a twelve-obstacle expedition and generated icons make magic a planned part of household and expedition play. Spell combinations and outcomes are implemented in game rules; no runtime LLM chooses spell powers. Optional dialogue features remain separate.

## How to play

Open **Work & study → Spellbook**. Select a person and an authored spell, learn its listed principles, choose testing components and spend two assigned phases testing it. Prepare learned spells within that person's preparation slots. Home castings take an assigned phase and the listed components; choose a recipient where offered. Expedition actions use the prepared spells carried by the party. The spell catalogue explains exact effects and limits before commitment.

Open **Rituals** for permanent circles. A conductor who knows both principles and a partner who knows at least one commit the listed materials and crowns, then work together for four to six phases. Assigning either elsewhere pauses the ritual. Resume explicitly, or cancel unfinished work to recover committed costs. Completed circles may be suspended and reactivated. Their effects begin after completion, not retroactively in that phase.

The **Cinder aqueduct** opens after Hearth wards research. Its twelve obstacles include fire, negotiation, flooding, underwater work, a gap, elemental and living enemies, a tower, undead, fallen stone, a construct and an inscription. Every obstacle has at least two ordinary approaches when adequately supplied. Prepared spells add alternatives. Choices exchange phases, components, money or vitality; a complete nonmagical route remains possible. Enemy damage and cleared obstacles persist, so retreat and recovery are viable. Unfinished aqueduct actions refund committed supplies on return; temporary field boosts end on return. Rewards are awarded once, only when brought home.

Threshold fold makes an outbound journey to a previously visited destination, or a return journey, immediate without advancing household work. First visits still require travel. The Anchor circle reduces its component cost.

Temporary work enchantments have finite charges and identical effects do not stack. Unused charges wait for matching work. Extra work is capped by the remaining project work where applicable. Casting itself occupies a worker phase, so preparation and who casts for whom matter.

## Icons and compatibility

Thirty spell icons and eight circle icons are individually generated with the built-in image-generation tool, in a shared colored-engraving style with transparent backgrounds. Runtime files are in `static/assets/v056/spells/`. Spellbook entries, guidance, expedition actions, work suggestions and ritual cards use them. Generation notes and the hash manifest are under `docs/`.

Save schema 47 initializes magic state and preserves existing spell names, progress, outfits and accepted art. Stop the old server; extract this release into a fresh folder; copy your complete existing `data` directory, including `data/assets`; run `python server.py`. The normal pre-migration database backup is made automatically. No offline phases elapse. Keep the v0.55 release and its backup for rollback; do not open a schema-47 database with older code.

## Verification scope

Python regression tests cover authored learning, secondary requirements, casting costs, healing, weaknesses, ordinary alternatives, retreat, instant travel, finite work boosts, shared rituals, migration and retry safety. Connected headless UI suites exercise the real controller and persistence bridge. Browser layout/visual QA remains deferred at the user's request. This release implements specific authored interactions, not an unrestricted physics or NPC simulation.
