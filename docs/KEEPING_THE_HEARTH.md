# Keeping the Hearth — Chapter 4, v0.78

Chapter 4 follows the completed Room to Grow walkthrough. The expanded castle now needs a dependable service entrance, defensive training, a watchroom and working dungeon facilities.

## Sabine and the service entrance

In a campaign that has not met Sabine, the vampire seal engraver is found attempting to take a seal impression tied to her inherited alarm oath. The initial encounter waits for an explicit response and steals nothing in the background. Challenge her, expose the passage with an owned spare warming lantern, or call a resident who is actually home. Inspect the latch, dark turn and broken bell cord afterward.

Sabine keeps her established identity, age, appearance, ambition, private-room preference and original containment case. If she is already known, contained, released, transferred or resident, the chapter begins as a security review. It does not invent a second crime, create another identity, reinstate an oath or recapture her.

## The defensive foundation

Complete these through ordinary headquarters construction:

- Entry hall and the existing warehouse/workshop prerequisites.
- Training yard and its once-only basic drill.
- Smithy and guard barracks.
- Underground quarters and dungeon ward.
- One entrance-defense installation: **shuttered watch post and sound gates**, or **warning seals and a signal bell**. Each costs 18 crowns and three assigned headquarters phases.
- One fitted Quiet chamber, using its normal crown/material costs and protected reserves.

Existing completed rooms and training count. The chapter offers already funded jobs before new funding. Residents can use their ordinary agreed headquarters jobs; the guide's default builder is the scholar. Work, pause/resume, travel, refunds and forecasts retain their normal rules. The barracks dormitory is fitted separately; no guard or bedroom is assigned automatically.

The two defensive installations produce distinct outcomes in the return scene. They are fixed protections for the authored encounter, not a recurring raid or passive-income system.

## Capture, care and recruitment tutorial

After completing the preparations, resolve the return encounter. Capture Sabine with controlled training, negotiate a safe escort, or drive her away. Capture and negotiated escort both use the existing admission system: reserve an unoccupied compatible Quiet chamber, establish Sabine's identity once, and bring her in on Advance. Chapter-specific account text connects the attempted theft to her existing oath without replacing her profile or the original archival encounter.

The guide walks through four recorded lessons:

1. Specialized chamber capacity is separate from residential beds.
2. Hear her account, agree the practical plan, learn the required preservation knowledge and fund the actual care project.
3. Resolve and release, or arrange a free specialist transfer; pending departure continues to reserve the chamber until Advance.
4. After release, ordinary correspondence, a private-room visit, her stay decision and the household invitation form a separate recruitment process.

Safe transfer remains available during unresolved care and refunds that project's exact held costs. Driving her away leaves the original archive encounter available; the tutorial then explains the empty facilities without inventing a prisoner. Existing cases use their saved status and accomplishments. Completion requires the defensive foundation, resolved incident and four lessons; recruiting Sabine is optional.

## Sabine as a unique resident

All existing content remains: **The second entrance** personal quest, procedural requests, personal story packages, household chapters, wardrobe invitations and three portrait ensembles, relationship/romance controls, ordinary research/work/training, expeditions and existing group stories.

New content:

- **A key with an owner**, a remembered dungeon conversation, and **An obligation crossed out**, its later shared-research follow-up.
- **Sabine's release-key register**, a 28-crown, three-phase dungeon specialty. Sabine must be a resident at home and have shared her first dungeon conversation to install it. Newly funded specialized chamber and care projects take one fewer scholar phase, minimum one. Already funded durations, costs, refunds, compatibility and release rules remain unchanged. The installed improvement remains useful as other permanent specialist installations do.
- **The key that comes back**, an additional two-obstacle personal quest involving old instructions and a release mechanism. It uses the existing patient, skilled and magical quest routes, grants normal once-only advancement and a named keepsake, and leaves the original quest untouched.
- Three specialist conversations covering planning, installation and later use.
- A repeatable ward-desk social activity and a two-part watchroom interaction with Aurelia.
- A dungeon-associated morning routine with library fallback; a private bedroom remains her home.

Memories use the existing journal and scoped dialogue context. A speaker receives only Chapter 4 memories in which they participated. Containment itself still does not grant access to resident or romance systems.
