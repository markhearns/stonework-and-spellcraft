## Playing a goal

Use Overview or Talk to see the ambition, then open Quests. Each work stage begins with two specific responses and records the selected approach. Both responses can lead to success; they are not hidden romance gates. There are 65 work stages, 130 stage responses, 32 ending responses and 32 post-completion questions. The two responses at a stage usually change the agreed approach and conversation, not the ultimate reward. Sabine’s necklace settlement also changes its actual price: 12 crowns to use the registry settlement, or 32 for a direct purchase.

Ordinary work takes three assigned phases, or two with existing applicable support. Trained work takes one phase when the existing attribute/skill check qualifies. Eligible prepared spells take one phase and cost their components in addition to stage supplies. No spell replaces a required practical test or voluntary agreement. Kaede’s tournament is an authored quest with practice and bout results resolved by assigned work; it does not introduce a separate live-combat tournament mode. All tournament stages forbid spell shortcuts.

The goal’s room must be restored before its work starts. Emberline also requires the enchanting room for inscription and proof stages. Both participants must be at home and available. Starting work pays the displayed crowns and unreserved materials once. Talking is free and does not advance time. Pausing preserves work and supplies already spent. The shared quest engine allows one active quest at a time.

## Lasting results

Every success gives the founder and companion two advancement once, records the completed result and offers two new questions. Profile facts and optional dialogue context acknowledge completion. The associated room shows the finished work. Re-reading and revisiting never re-award equipment, advancement or relationship gains.

- Zahra: Emberline, an owned steel sword with two enchantment capacity and rank-one Measured force installed. Equip and select enchantments through the ordinary equipment controls.
- Sabine: her recovered fox-clasp necklace, an owned pendant. No unlisted magical bonus.
- Elowen: one three-treatment field kit plus a restocking recipe. During a manticore, wyvern or basilisk encounter, spend one kit through First aid to heal up to four vitality and clear venom/stone stiffness for one member. It takes the normal exchange and enemy retaliation still applies. An unperformed pending action is refunded on retreat. Restock at the infirmary for three unreserved silver ivy and one binding thread, up to ten kits.
- Tamsin: select her four-dish menu from the cookbook panel or pantry. It appears in actual morning meal records, costs normal provisions and provides no extra instant meal or statistical bonus.
- Velis: the western-market route is separate from Chapter 6’s North watch road. It provides up to two extra free provisions at morning, capped by the pantry target and added to existing supply contracts.
- All other results: persistent named completed works and follow-up conversations. They do not silently grant extra combat, income or relationship modifiers. Neris’s grotto is an improvement beside the existing hot spring, not an additional map room.

## Existing quests and saves

Schema 76 adds a separate companion-goal record without replacing earlier history. Completed earlier quests credit Mira’s first two stages, Merrin’s first two stages or Koharu’s first stage. An accepted overlapping quest must be finished before its new goal starts. A new goal suppresses an unstarted overlapping old invitation. Other shared outings and signature projects retain their separate purpose.

Cheats can recruit all 16 named companions, including Merrin. Recruitment does not complete their ambitions. The ordinary room/material cheats can help prepare a test campaign; goals still use their real decisions and work controls.

## Merrin’s physical presence

Her introductory and repeatable conversations explain that she is massless but can exert force by concentrating. Familiar actions become easy; heavy or precise unfamiliar work needs attention and breaks. Her clothes and bell belong to her manifested form; books and ordinary objects remain physical. The binding scene applies this distinction to actual work. Her condition confers no new lock bypass, injury immunity or assumed consent. After the comedy is completed, unplayed overlapping household scenes use the completed copy and do not send the player looking for it again.
