# Specialized chambers and voluntary transitions — v0.30

This is a bounded authored implementation of design section 11, reached from **Specialized chambers**. It is not a random capture system or a general procedural encounter generator.

## Capacity and safety

Ten fixed one-person chambers can be restored: five Ember chambers for heat instability and five Quiet chambers for oath echoes. Each costs 14 crowns, two unreserved porous clay and one unreserved binding thread, and takes two of the scholar's own primary phases. A Proper Living Wing is required first. Chamber capacity is completely separate from residential beds and the estate's 25 + 25 resident ceilings.

Only compatible ready vacant chambers can accept an escort. The chamber is reserved immediately; arrival happens on explicit Advance. Food, bedding, safety and care require neither work nor intimacy. There are no random failures, attacks on the living wing, forced labor, affection meters or romance rewards. An occupied specialized chamber cannot be assigned as a bedroom.

One chamber/care project can be committed at a time. Assigning another primary activity pauses it. Skill, focus and attribute bonuses do not accelerate its required phases. Cancellation refunds the exact saved committed costs once; completed work cannot be refunded.

## Two authored cases

They have distinct ambitions, personalities, voices, appearances and room preferences. Their adult identities are initialized once with ordinary zero-rank build records and no free advancement. Resolving their situations preserves personality and does not manufacture loyalty or romantic interest. The scholar receives two advancement once for each resolved case.

These encounters are introduced through authored reports and safe warder escorts; an interactive hostile field-encounter sequence is not implemented here.

## Independent decisions

Direct requests to reopen a closed care contact, invite, recruit, flirt through the normal dialogue route or assign work cannot bypass containment. Specialist transfer is not treated as successful threat resolution or an automatic recruitment opportunity.

## Persistence and art

Bundled portraits are ordinary adult character references, not depictions of captivity. Their neutral scenes, muted palette and handmade surface follow the established aesthetic. See `../CONTAINMENT_ART.md` for paths and exact prompts. Imported corrections use the existing review and rollback workflow without changing mechanics.

## Verification and remaining scope

Rules tests cover all ten chamber slots, compatible escort reservations, phased arrival, independent normal housing, protected materials, costs, exact refunds, pause/resume, absence, case prerequisites, no identity rewrite, one-time awards, no recruitment bypass, unconditional release, private room preference, membership and same-person return. The connected UI suite exercises care/release/recruitment and a separate unfinished-care transfer/refund, portrait display and reload.

Remaining scope includes additional cases, field encounter integration, specialist follow-up, more chamber types, post-release personal stories and actual rendered-browser review. The specialized chamber screen is a management ledger, not a new illustrated dungeon map.
