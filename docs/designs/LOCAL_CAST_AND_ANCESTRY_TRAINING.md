Pool version 2 adds these choices without rerolling existing identities. Bovinefolk, orcs and wolfkin cannot enter through summoning. Oni remain exotic. Personality, values and willingness are independent of ancestry. Existing elf, kitsune and golem distinctions are preserved.

## Four illustrated, persistent people

| Person | Age | Lead | Result of introduction |

| Fenna, wolfkin route keeper | 22 | Return with a fern-nursery discovery | Ordinary remote contact |
| Kaede, oni glassworker | 24 | Record Courteous passage | Named exotic plan; normal summoning ritual still required |

Use **Local introductions**. Each appointment or letter study occupies the scholar's primary assignment for one explicit Advance. It costs no crowns or materials. Pause and cancellation preserve the lead. Meetings never occupy a bedroom or recruit someone automatically. The common contacts use normal conversations, agreed visits, bed checks and two-sided membership. Kaede's letter is not a free summoning shortcut.

These are authored introductory encounters, not a procedural settlement simulation or arbitrary expedition-party encounter engine. Leads reference actual saved unlocks; they do not grant discoveries. The four named portraits belong to these people only. Their definitions are frozen into the save when introduced, and their histories persist after departure. They do not consume one of the fifty generated-plan slots. Existing housing and regional ceilings still apply.

Names are reserved for future generated proposals. If an older custom identity already uses a newly authored name, its identity is preserved and the conflicting authored introduction is unavailable; no automatic rename occurs.

## Earned ancestry training

Physical traits are expressed through optional training rather than hidden starting work bonuses. Each path costs **3 earned personal advancement and 2 own training phases**, using the established training, cancellation, investment and retraining systems. Starting rank, skill and advancement budgets remain unchanged.

| Path | Eligible ancestry | Prerequisites | Exact effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Practised strength | Bovinefolk or Oni | Dexterity 2, Artifice 1, Hearth affinity 1 | +1 crafting work contribution |
| Enduring focus | Orc | Resolve 2, Scholarship 1, Hearth affinity 1 | +1 personal-story work contribution per assigned phase, capped at remaining work |
| Practised keen observation | Wolfkin | Insight 2, Scholarship 1, Light affinity 1 | +1 research/archive work contribution |

No extra action, automatic discovery, affection, obedience or infinite resource effect. Strength does not shorten construction, training, travel or personal stories. Endurance does not accelerate other work. Keen observation does not reveal hidden lore. Contributions appear in the appropriate existing work breakdown or story forecast. Other ancestries retain their ordinary attribute, affinity and perk choices. No broad combat/endurance statistic system is claimed.

## Stories that build on shared history

Two additional bounded packages extend the original four:

| Chapter | Unlock | Cost | Base work | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| A shared revision | Complete any personal story and actually join its follow-up | 14 crowns, 2 binding thread, 1 porous clay | 3 | 2 personal advancement |
| A collected working method | Complete and share A shared revision; at least two completed personal stories | 18 crowns, 3 binding thread, 2 porous clay | 4 | 2 personal advancement |

Each can be accepted only once per person. Offline outlines name the earlier completed work that was actually shared. Approval snapshots references to those earlier records. Model-written proposals receive the same bounded package availability and owner-scoped continuity. A preview, deferred invitation or unfinished project does not satisfy the prerequisite. Joining or rereading a scene grants no reward. Existing funding, reserves, pause/cancel/refund and departure protections continue to apply.
