# Reviewed personal stories — v0.31

Every current NPC household member, including generated ordinary recruits, exotic recruits and awakened golems, can receive an optional reviewed personal story. Visitors must first independently choose membership. Founder, Eris and Selene are excluded: the gamemaster does not write external players' personal arcs.

The Personal stories page provides offline outlines and optional provider-written proposals. Structured drafts are durable, recoverable and explicitly reviewed for both content and rules. Approval creates an unfunded offer; it does not charge funds, advance time, assign work, or narrate the proposed completion as already true. A stale proposal must be rechecked against the current save without another provider call.

## Bounded packages

| Package | Crowns | Materials | Own phases | Completion |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| Preservation notebook | 10 | 2 binding thread, 1 porous clay | 3 | 2 personal advancement; Gentle preservation |
| Useful light notebook | 12 | 1 fireglass, 1 binding thread | 3 | 2 personal advancement; Gentle refraction |
| Guided water notebook | 10 | 2 porous clay, 1 silver ivy | 3 | 2 personal advancement; Water guidance |
| Personal craft folio | 8 | 1 binding thread, 1 porous clay | 2 | 1 personal advancement |

A principle package is available only when the principle is already in the archive or personally understood by the owner. Each package may be accepted once per person, including deferred offers; awards have stable per-person keys. These arcs cannot bypass research or create infinite advancement.

Funding respects material reserves, commits the exact visible costs, and assigns the owner's primary work to her story. One active story per person. Other work pauses. Progress occurs only on Advance while she is home and assigned; the scholar need not remain home after agreement. Pausing preserves progress. Cancellation refunds exact committed funds and materials once and resets unfinished work to the offer. Funded work blocks an agreed departure until finished or cancelled.

Completion records the accomplishment, grants only the package reward, and opens an optional non-expiring follow-up. Joining, deferring, restoring or rereading a scene spends no phase or resources and changes no relationship, Resonance or rewards. The scene is added once to her own conversation. The current accepted portrait path is captured for continuity; no new scene illustration is generated.

Provider context includes that NPC's public identity, ambition, personal understanding, own completed arcs and explicitly shared castle evidence. It excludes raw campaign state, hidden castle lore and others' private dialogue. Ordinary dialogue context receives her own completed arcs and only follow-up scenes actually joined. The eight curated story seeds have distinct offline follow-ups; authored residents have a generic offline outline or can use reviewed provider prose.

## Limits

Four bounded package families, not a complete generative campaign director. Human review remains responsible for natural-language coherence. No live-provider verification is claimed. Generated portraits and clothing remain separate reviewed artwork operations.
