# Curated characters and distinct arrival paths — v0.31

The generator composes a reviewed identity from versioned ingredients. It does not reroll an established person or use generated prose as executable rules. Authored residents keep their distinct identities and portraits.

High elves are fair-skinned forest elves. Dark elves have chocolate-brown skin and an elegant visual direction. Drow come from subterranean settlements; the offline pool uses slate, charcoal or violet-grey skin. No morality, cruelty or allegiance is assigned by ancestry. Catfolk have feline ears and tails. Kitsune have fox-spirit traits; the initial offline representation has one tail, which gives no free power. More subtypes and tail configurations remain future content. Dwarves, halflings and gnomes are not implemented.

Seraph replaces Angel. Migration updates the structured ancestry and authored Aurelia origin while preserving identity, accepted art, progress and player-written conversation history. Older saved Angel proposals normalize to Seraph when reviewed. Aurelia retains her warm tan portrait.

## Ingredients and generation

Eight backgrounds, eight temperaments, eight story seeds, varied adult appearances and three starting capability packages are combined through explicit ancestry/background/story compatibility. Automatic selection weights less-used saved roster ingredients. This encourages variety; it is not a promise that every generated person will be unique in every detail.

The default automatic route is exotic summoning. Selecting a common ancestry or Golem infers its proper route; an explicitly conflicting route is rejected before a provider request. Explicit body material selections are only valid for golems.

Offline composition needs no provider configuration and ignores the optional writing brief. Model-written proposals receive selected ingredients, public identities and a user brief. Exact ancestry, adult age/form age, occupation and capability package are checked; nuanced personality, visual fidelity and prose coherence require the explicit human review already shown in the interface. No semantic validator is claimed.

A request ID fixes selections and result recovery. A retry does not spend another provider request or select another identity. A new draft deliberately requests another combination. Approval stores ingredient provenance; existing accepted people are never reconstructed from the current pool. Fifty enduring reviewed plans remain the current campaign limit.

## Starting capability

Every package has one starting principle, one practice and a personal focus. Added skill ranks and earned advancement start at zero; attributes and affinities use the normal character initialization. An ancestry, tail, wing, crafted body, background sentence or story cannot grant hidden powers, labor bonuses, funds, extra turns or affection. The three packages are Archive reader, Light maker and Water worker.

## Golem construction and awakening

A proposal describes an adult feminine form, independent personality and prospective ambition. The displayed 18–25 value is **adult-form age**, not lived years. The person awakens fully adult in cognition and presentation; there is no infant or child stage and no invented previous life. The saved awakening date records her beginning.

Crowns cover base body supplies; inventory components serve assembly, binding and the animating focus. These are provisional balance numbers. Materials currently change description and costs, not mechanical bonuses. The approved body rules are saved with the plan.

The scholar must personally know Clear instruction and Gentle preservation. One construction project can be active. It uses the scholar's primary assignment and progresses only on explicit Advance, without skill/focus acceleration. Pausing preserves progress. Cancelling an unawakened plan refunds exact committed costs once; spent time is not returned. A cancelled plan may be funded again.

Accommodation is planned but not reserved during construction. On the final awakening phase, the game rechecks a suitable free bedroom and regional capacity. If unavailable, work waits at five completed phases without extra cost. Successful awakening occupies the bed and opens the shared visitor/conversation lifecycle. The golem is a guest, receives no automatic assignment or allowance, and is not automatically a household member. Her own stay decision and the household decision are separate. Departure preserves her identity and belongings. Awakened people cannot be cancelled, refunded or rebuilt.

## Limits

No automatic portrait generation, appearance editor, ancestry subtypes or arbitrary model-generated abilities. Newly generated identities use the existing reviewed portrait-import path. The current offline prose catalogue is deliberately finite. Co-op and real external-agent authority remain unfinished.
