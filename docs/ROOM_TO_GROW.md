# Room to Grow — Chapter 3, v0.77

Chapter 1 establishes a habitable home. Chapter 2 gives its archive, garden and workshop a chosen purpose. Chapter 3 restores a coherent lower living wing around those working rooms.

## Completion and continuity

For campaigns enrolled in The First Hearth, its closing choice comes before Chapter 2. That choice still links to the player's chosen interest; an additional link opens Chapter 2. Older campaigns without the opening chapter qualify through their existing living wing and hearth evidence.

Chapter 2 requires all three undertakings, in any order. Each retains its own design, actual contributors, gathering, ordinary-use demonstration and concluding memory. Previously discovered evidence and delivered requests count without repeated rewards. After the third undertaking, **Three rooms, one home** credits all recorded construction and opens Chapter 3. Existing completed undertakings count immediately; the retrospective is an explicit, once-only action.

The restored underground quarters supply dry access, ventilation, shared sitting space and warded services. Their existing washroom and knowledge requirements still apply. Dependencies such as the warehouse and workshop appear once in the plan. Already restored rooms count.

Before saving, compare the exact remaining crown cost, already committed funds, catalogue total and base work remaining. Saving the scope spends nothing, moves nobody and assigns no work. The scope stays fixed for the chapter; priorities and the next builder can change afterward. Further rooms remain available through ordinary construction.

The plan is part of Home and Headquarters, with direct navigation and search entries. It offers one next action, shows dependencies and assigned builders, and links to ordinary room controls. Paid work is offered before new work. Reordering priorities preserves every paid project, worker and progress value. If the selected builder already has another funded headquarters job, the guide offers that job first. Workers still need their own knowledge and explicit work agreement. The scholar fits bedrooms under the established housing rules; agreed residents may build headquarters rooms in parallel through the existing controls.

Copying guidance uses actual income and the current shortfall. It never grants resources or starts a queue. Funding explicitly switches the chosen worker's assignment. Cancel/refund, travel, phase forecasts and work arrangements remain owned by the existing construction systems.

## A completed wing

Once every planned room and service is ready, record four walkthrough stops: services, bedrooms, the communal room and the specialist facility. Close with a quiet reflection or a gathering of residents actually at home. Neither choice advances time or grants an extra reward.

The chapter records observed work deltas by room and named builder, including work spent before a later cancellation. Existing construction gets no invented contributor. Ordinary room benefits and usable beds are the rewards. Empty beds can remain empty; nobody is invited, moved or assigned a profession automatically. The plan surfaces existing residents' explicit private-room preferences as optional planning information.

The opening recalls the actual three Chapter 2 designs. The ending connects the first hearth, working rooms and expanded household. Memories appear in the existing journal. NPC dialogue receives only development memories in which that speaker participated; private walkthrough notes and unseen history remain private.
