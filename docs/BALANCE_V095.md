# v0.95 balance check

The audit used 84 deterministic battle fixtures: seven solo/group configurations, all six enemy types, and baseline versus trained builds. Fixtures use ordinary steel equipment and leather protection. Trained fixtures also prepare authored techniques and completed signature prerequisites; these are controlled test setups, not earned progression. A simple policy reads the same previews exposed to the player, values damage, healing and avoiding incapacitation, and takes no peaceful shortcuts. Results do not prove optimal play.

| Scenario group | Completed battles |
| --- | ---: |
| Baseline builds | 33/42 |
| Trained builds | 35/42 |
| All two-person/four-person group cases | 48/48 |

Baseline solo characters struggled against bandits and mythical enemies. Trained Kaede and Sabine improved against bandits; basic solo mythical encounters remained dangerous. Retreat remains available and has no equipment loss or permanent injury. The supply-road description now warns that armed bandits may require a solo retreat.

Across group cases, prepared builds reduced average outing duration from 6.83 to 5.79 advances, and average total vitality loss from 5.17 to 1.92. Protection, control and healing were actually used, rather than merely listed as available. A prepared group benefits from its build without making an ordinary steel loadout irrelevant.

The continuous Chapter 1–8 tests used earned resources, saved/reloaded every action and no cheats. With a policy that responds to the visible intent, Chapter 8 took 27 advances/9 days on the negotiation route and 30 advances/10 days on the detour route. Required nights at home and the final supper remained enforced. These are route measurements, not promised human playtime.

Repeat rewards and the six enemy-type advancement limit were unchanged. Signature growth requires breadth of resolved encounters and paid work, so endlessly repeating wolves does not unlock higher refinement ranks.
